# v0.2 execution notes

The first frozen-code invocation stopped before producing or inspecting outcome metrics. During the first fit's evaluation, the three-party audit control mistakenly partitioned the same world's evidence once per query, exceeding the existing per-party padding limit. The bug was confined to audit-control data assembly; the training objective, model, held-out corpus, and decision rules did not change. The failed process's partial writer checkpoint was discarded. The failure log is retained as `run-attempt-1.log`.

The corrected control partitions each world's evidence once and broadcasts those three party states across that world's four queries. Before restart, the counted sampler was rechecked against the frozen v0.1 sampler on six fixtures, and the evaluator was exercised on 60 held-out-style worlds across all registered overlap strata. MAXSET, three-party trees, probabilities, private labels, and capacity bounds passed. The corrected source and the original preregistration/configuration are rehashed in `FREEZE.json` before restarting the complete run.

The corrected full invocation completed all six writer fits and all held-out inference, writing the complete prediction and probability archive. It then stopped at aggregate JSON serialization because NumPy boolean scalars were not converted to native JSON booleans. No aggregate metrics were printed or inspected from that failed process. `summarize_saved.py` deterministically aggregates the already-saved predictions without retraining or rerunning inference; its SHA-256 and the resulting `results.json` are recorded in `POSTHOC.json`.

The final methodological audit found one protocol mismatch: the preregistration requested a fresh RNG seed at every training step, while the implementation used one continuous seeded stream per fit. The arms remained exactly paired. This is recorded as D1 in `DEVIATIONS.md` and narrows the result's confirmatory interpretation; it does not justify silently rewriting the frozen preregistration.
