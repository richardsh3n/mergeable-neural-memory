## Explicit separation from the first proposal round

The first round asks (1) what causal information a bounded mergeable state must preserve, (2) which existing memories to evict under future decision value, and (3) how to revoke one source after shared compression. Round two instead studies (1) future-intention lifecycle and action, (2) the causal feedback between memory-conditioned action and the evidence later observed, and (3) the choice to acquire evidence before it can be written. Proposal 3 has some resource-allocation adjacency to decision-value forgetting, so its main endpoint must be acquisition value and cross-task transfer—not eviction quality or bytes retained alone.

## Search procedure and limitations

Searches were run against arXiv, ACL Anthology, PMLR, and OpenReview-indexed results with combinations of “prospective memory,” “intention store,” “partial observability,” “experience following,” “active memory,” “memory controller,” “active exploration,” and “cross-episode transfer.” Primary venue/arXiv pages were used for cited claims. This was targeted browsing, not a database-exported systematic review: no PRISMA protocol, duplicate screening, citation chasing to saturation, code-level reproducibility audit, or conference-revision reconciliation has been performed. The titles and abstracts establish proximity, not equivalence or absence of other work.
